# Electus interface

Electus is a recruiting workspace for importing CVs, finding relevant experience, and tracking candidate reviews.

- Use Inter, low-contrast surface borders, neutral backgrounds, and emerald for the primary action and current selection. Light is the default for new users. Existing theme preferences still apply.
- Keep the workspace navigation on the left at desktop widths and above content on phones.
- Lead with candidate totals, pending and reviewed counts, and the percentage reviewed. Search and filters change the candidate view while the summary stays at workspace scope.
- Keep candidate actions alongside the relevant row. Group destructive bulk actions in a menu and confirm them before sending a request.
- Show measured candidate data in analytics. Date ranges use the CV upload date. Keep sample billing figures and processing times out of operational views.
- Use responsive sections and visible keyboard focus. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
